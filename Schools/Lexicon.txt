TERM: Interaction
--------------------------------------------------

Definition:
measurable state-affecting relation.

Purpose:
root causal primitive for all CE-OS interaction analysis.

Dependencies:
none

Admissible Uses:
- measurable influence
- observable state relation
- representable system effect

Non-Admissible Uses:
- mystical connectedness
- undefined universal linkage

Recovery Compression:
interaction = measurable state-affecting relation
