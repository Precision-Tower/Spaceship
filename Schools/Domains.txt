==================================================
1. DOMAIN PURPOSE
==================================================

Domains exist to preserve:
- interaction identity
- behavior classification
- propagation boundaries
- coupling specificity
- loss identity
- cross-domain traceability

Domain separation required because:
interaction classes are not semantically interchangeable.

Cross-domain collapse causes:
- propagation broadening
- field universalization
- transfer ambiguity
- undefined coupling
- semantic averaging

Primary Stability Principle:
domain identity must survive abstraction and compression.

6. PROPAGATION VS TRANSFER TAXONOMY
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
6.1 Propagation
--------------------------------------------------

Propagation:
field-centric distributed gradient interaction behavior.

Requires:
- field-centric identity
- measurable gradient structure
- distributed interaction behavior

Examples:
- electromagnetic propagation
- radiative field behavior
- distributed field interaction

Propagation Forbidden:
- generic movement
- pressure transfer
- load passage
- mass displacement
- generalized transmission

--------------------------------------------------
6.2 Transfer
--------------------------------------------------

Transfer:
movement or passage of:
- load
- pressure
- displacement
- momentum
- state conditions

Transfer does NOT require:
field-centric distributed gradients.

--------------------------------------------------
6.3 Transmission
--------------------------------------------------

Transmission:
organized passage of interaction conditions through constrained pathways.

Transmission may occur in:
- mechanical systems
- fluid systems
- structural systems
- conductive systems

Transmission does NOT automatically imply:
field propagation.

--------------------------------------------------
6.4 Displacement
--------------------------------------------------

Displacement:
change in position/state within mass-centric or structural conditions.

Displacement
≠ propagation

--------------------------------------------------
6.5 Conductive Interaction
--------------------------------------------------

Conductive Interaction:
interaction behavior through structured conductive pathways.

Conductive interaction
≠ electromagnetic propagation through space.

--------------------------------------------------
6.6 Electromagnetic Propagation
--------------------------------------------------

Electromagnetic Propagation:
field-centric distributed interaction behavior through measurable field gradients.

Electromagnetic propagation
≠ conductive electrical transport
