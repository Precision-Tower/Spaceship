==================================================
1. GOVERNANCE PURPOSE
==================================================

Governance exists because:
GPT systems naturally:
- average distinctions
- flatten certainty levels
- broaden abstractions
- merge neighboring concepts
- smooth semantic boundaries
- recursively reinterpret compressed concepts

Ontology alone is insufficient because:
definitions without constraints drift under:
- summarization
- paraphrase
- truncation
- partial retrieval
- multi-agent interpretation

Governance objective:
preserve:
- distinction topology
- admissibility boundaries
- dependency integrity
- authority stability
- reconstruction fidelity

Governance priority:
constraint preservation
>
interpretive flexibility

4. CLAIM TIERING SYSTEM
==================================================

Tier-0:
Interpretive Framing
= conceptual interpretation without engineering assertion.

Tier-1:
Conceptual Hypothesis
= structured but unvalidated explanatory proposal.

Tier-2:
Engineering Implication
= possible engineering consequence inferred from interpretation.

Tier-3:
Experimental Proposal
= testable procedural claim.

Tier-4:
Observed Anomaly
= measured deviation requiring investigation.

Tier-5:
Demonstrated Capability
= repeatable observed engineering behavior.

Tier-6:
Validated Engineering Behavior
= independently verified operational behavior.

--------------------------------------------------
4.1 Escalation Rules
--------------------------------------------------

Tier escalation requires:
- explicit evidentiary transition
- measurable support
- preserved claim boundaries

Forbidden:
interpretation
→ implied validation
→ asserted capability

--------------------------------------------------
4.2 GPT Escalation Constraint
--------------------------------------------------

GPT systems must NOT:
- imply validation rhetorically
- elevate plausibility into capability
- treat interpretation as proof
- infer engineering success from conceptual coherence
