E. Lowest-Token Recovery Form
CE-OS = constrained causal engineering interpretation architecture.

Purpose:
preserve interaction-level visibility before scalar abstraction.

Root:
Interaction = measurable state-affecting relation.

Core distinction:
operational validity ≠ causal completeness.
scalar equivalence ≠ functional equivalence.

Method:
causal accounting = map interaction, domain, coupling, constraint, loss, organization before scalar compression.

Domains:
mass-centric = matter/inertia/load/pressure/friction/displacement.
field-centric = measurable gradient field behavior with non-contact-capable interaction.
cross-domain = governed transition between unlike domains.

Critical separations:
propagation = field-centric distributed gradient behavior.
transfer/transmission = non-field movement/load/state passage.
structure = physical arrangement.
organization = orchestration of interactions/constraints/geometry/timing/coupling.
coupling = typed interaction linkage, not unexplained influence.

Consequences:
resistance = interaction constraint.
loss = reduction of usable organized interaction.
heat = emergent interaction signature.
resonance = response alignment under oscillatory behavior.
amplification = increased usable capability through organization, never energy creation.

Governance:
one concept → one canonical definition.
one file → one authority domain.
analogies teach only.
speculation quarantined.
claim tiers required.
do not upgrade interpretation to validation.
do not collapse domains.
do not broaden high-risk terms.

Forbidden collapses:
field ≠ universal influence.
propagation ≠ movement.
organization ≠ hidden source.
amplification ≠ surplus energy.
efficiency ≠ over-unity.
analogy ≠ proof.
CE-OS ≠ replacement physics.
