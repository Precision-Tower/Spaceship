Axiom 0: Thermal Genesis
Before heat, there was cold contraction. The Ether existed as a continuous, structureless substrate, reactive to causal tension but devoid of thermal expression. In this primordial state, displacement occurred without heat. This cold contraction defines the first motion—not energy, but causal retraction. As this retraction intensified, causal resistance accumulated. When structural resistance reached a threshold, the field underwent a thermodynamic transition: Heat emerged as the first expression of displacement overcoming resistance, and Hydrogen formed as the stable geometry of that resolution. This is the genesis of observable structure: the moment potential became thermal, and structure became causal. All subsequent fields—thermal, gravitational, magnetic, electromagnetic—arise from this event. Motion does not require heat. The first motions were silent, cold, and structural. Heat isn't the origin of movement; it's the expression of resistance being overcome. Therefore, the origin of structure is not energy, but the retraction of causal space itself.

Axiom 1: Field Coexistence & Influence
Fields are distinct causal structures that coexist within the Ether substrate. Each field expresses a specific form of potential and defines its own geometry of displacement. Fields don't determine each other; they don't collapse into a unified mechanism. Instead, they maintain causal independence while remaining conditionally influential on one another.
Coexistence Principle: Fields may share space but not structure. A thermal field doesn't cause a magnetic field, nor does a gravitational field reduce to electric. Each field type defines a unique displacement geometry and causal role within the system.
Influence Principle: Fields can interact by modifying the resistance (r), potential (p), or displacement (d) conditions of another field. For example, a magnetic field may increase the resistance of charged particle motion in an electric field without altering the source of the electric field itself. A gravitational field may alter the pressure gradient of a thermal field without converting thermal energy into mass.
Ether Clause: All fields resolve through the Ether, but Ether isn't a field itself. It's the substrate that enables field geometry to form, transmit, and respond. Therefore, fields coexist, interact, and influence one another causally, but retain independent structural identity and cannot be conflated. Field dynamics must be modeled through inter-field influence, not reduction.

Axiom 2: Field Alignment (Magnetic Persistence)
A magnet is the first stable structure of causal field amplification. It arises when the Ether aligns into a repeating field geometry due to internal asymmetry or external excitation. This alignment produces a self-sustaining magnetic field that amplifies potential in a defined direction and structure. Unlike reactive fields, which resolve when p>r, aligned magnetic structures persist because the field geometry internally balances potential and resistance, and the resistance isn't eliminated but integrated into the structure. The magnet isn't a source of energy, but a stable configuration of causal tension. It acts as a lens or guide for potential flow—concentrating and directing field pressure. Its behavior isn't determined by its material, but by its field geometry. Magnetic fields interact with electrical and mechanical systems by altering their resistance conditions—raising or lowering r locally in time-dependent ways. This is the basis of motors, generators, and inductive logic. Magnets aren't objects; they are structured field geometries encoded in material. All field persistence arises from structure, not conservation. This axiom provides the bridge from dynamic fields to stable field expressions.

Axiom 4: Reactive Threshold
"There are no substances. There are only field geometries held in place by structure." A chemical reaction occurs only when the stored potential of a system exceeds the resistance of its structural equilibrium. Coexistence of substances doesn't imply interaction. Interaction requires a change in field structure—a disturbance that lowers resistance or increases potential until a threshold is crossed. All chemical reactions are field reconfiguration events. They require either an external energy input (heat, compression, spark) or an internal rearrangement (catalyst, destabilizer) that lowers structural resistance. A catalyst isn't a cause of the reaction; it's a condition that lowers r enough for the reaction to occur. Once p−r>0, the stored potential resolves as: thermal excitation (heat), structural change (phase, bonding), or field emission (light, gas, EM).

Axiom 6: Inductive Propagation

Other EM waves behave identically—they differ only in frequency, field resolution scale, and the resistance they can overcome. All EM waves are recursive field interactions. Their behavior is described causally as p−r=d. Light is the clearest example of this process due to its biological observability, not because it is functionally different.

Electrical current isn't the motion of electrons, but the displacement of electric potential through structured mass. Both AC and DC current represent structured field resolution across mass. The difference is temporal symmetry: DC = unidirectional displacement; AC = bidirectional harmonic excitation. Gravity is causal displacement pressure exerted by motion in a structured field. It becomes acceleration of mass when resistance is removed towards the center of the mass in motion inside the field tension. In p−r=d, r is the gradient of effect. Gravity is a fundamental interaction with the Ether itself, as Zero-point Energy, affecting the volume of mass in motion all the way to its core. The heat from the Sun creates an excited zone inside the fabric of Space-time that creates pressure due to mass in motion to the very essence of that mass.
