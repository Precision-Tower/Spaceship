**Kinetic Energy Model**
A Causal Framework for Understanding Motion, Pressure, and Energy
The Kinetic Energy Model proposes a fundamental shift in how we understand the universe's dynamics. It defines energy not as a conserved quantity, but as a causal resolution: potential (P) overcoming resistance (R) to produce displacement (D). This foundational relationship, P−R=D, applies universally across all physical domains.

**Core Philosophy**
Unlike traditional conservation-based physics, this model focuses on the causal chain of events. Energy is not a stored entity but the visible expression of this causal resolution. The model distinguishes sharply between:
Mass-Centric Systems: Where interactions are primarily governed by the properties and motion of mass (e.g., hydraulics, classical mechanics).
Field-Centric Systems: Where causal influence is transmitted through continuous spatial structures (e.g., magnetism, thermal radiation, gravity, electromagnetic phenomena).
The goal is not to derive predictive formulas based on conservation, but to construct precise causal chains: identifying the source of potential, quantifying the resistance, and observing or modeling the resulting displacement.

**Key Principles**
Energy as Resolution: Energy is the resolution of causal tension, not a stored entity.
Displacement (D) as Output: Displacement is the output of potential (P) resolved through resistance (R).
Domain Integrity: Each domain (mass, field, fluid, thermal) must preserve its causal logic; no cross-domain assumptions without a defined causal bridge.
Causal Chains: Kinetic systems are expressed as a sequence of resolution events, not collapsed abstractions.
Resolution, Not Conservation: The model tracks how energy transfers form and resolves, rather than relying on conservation-based shortcuts.

**Model Structure**
This repository outlines the Kinetic Energy Model through a structured documentation approach:
Definitions: A comprehensive glossary of all terms used within the model, from universal causal concepts (Potential, Resistance, Displacement) to domain-specific expressions (Mass, Field, Thermal Field, Magnetic Field, Electrical Energy, etc.). These are the foundational vocabulary.
Axioms: The primitive, causally primitive truths from which all other theorems and observations are derived. These axioms establish the fundamental operations and interactions within the model's universe.
Theorems: Derived logical consequences and explanations of observed phenomena, built by integrating two or more axioms. This section will demonstrate the model's explanatory power for complex systems like chemical reactions, stellar dynamics, and even the nature of life.
Applications: Practical examples and detailed causal analyses of real-world systems, demonstrating how the Kinetic Energy Model can be applied in engineering design, field theory modeling, and physics education.

**Goal**
The overarching goal of the Kinetic Energy Model is to provide a unified, causal framework that allows engineers, physicists, and system designers to model any kinetic system through domain-specific cause and resolution—not conservation math. It is not about what energy is, but about how energy resolves.
